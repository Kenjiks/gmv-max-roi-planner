Incremental ROI = extra GMV ÷ extra ad spend compared with your current setting. Extra spend pays for itself only when incremental ROI is above your break-even ROI.

How this works

Contribution margin = what you keep from each 1 of GMV after TikTok Shop fees, COGS, affiliate commission, refunds and per-order costs, before ads.

Break-even ROI = 1 ÷ contribution margin. Below this, ad spend costs more than the sales it brings in.

Growth ROI = the lowest target ROI that still keeps your minimum net profit margin. It gives the most GMV inside your profit floor.

Profit-max ROI = the target ROI that makes the most net profit. Below it you trade some profit for more GMV and market share.

Scaling: a lower ROI target lets GMV Max reach a wider audience. GMV rises, but ad spend rises faster. The TikTok benchmark curve is applied in proportion to your current ROI. Results outside the shaded range are extrapolated.

ROI guarantee: if a day delivers less than 90% of target ROI, GMV Max credits back the shortfall. The worst case is about 0.9 × target ROI, and the "worst-case profit" column assumes that.

Recommended rollout: lower ROI in steps (e.g. 3.6 → 3.5 → 3.3), hold each for 3–5 days, then compare the real results with this plan before the next step.

This is a planning estimate, not a guarantee of results. Actual performance depends on budget, creatives, stock, pricing and seasonality.
